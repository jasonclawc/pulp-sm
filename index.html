What lands in my mailbox?

Physical Pulp mail once a month: prints, stickers, and occasional extras. The archive artwork on this website is inspiration, not a preview of the final mailing.

Is it really $8, anywhere?

Yes. $8 USD per month includes worldwide postage. Any applicable taxes are shown at checkout. Available shipping destinations are shown when you enter your address.

Which mailing am I joining?

December’s mail club shipment has been sent. New members now join the January mailing, which goes out December 23, 2026. We send one envelope each month. December 23 is the dispatch date for January’s mailing; arrival times vary by destination.

How does the subscription work?

You pay when you join, then monthly on your billing date. One subscription means one envelope per month. You can cancel future renewals through the member portal; your membership continues through the period you've already paid for.

Moving? Need to change your card?

Use Manage membership below to update your shipping address, payment method, or subscription. If a mailing has already been prepared, send a message to @pulp.sm about an address change.

What happens to my information?

Stripe handles checkout and payment information. Pulp uses your contact and mailing details to manage your membership and send physical mail. We don't collect card details on this website. For membership questions, contact @pulp.sm.
