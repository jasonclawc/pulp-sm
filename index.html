What am I signing up for?

A recurring subscription to physical Pulp mail, delivered once a month. The planned mix is prints, stickers, and occasional extras. The launch mailing includes a limited edition founders print.

How much is it?

The membership is $8 USD per month, including worldwide postage. Any applicable taxes will be shown before you pay. Paid subscriptions are not open yet.

Can I join from outside the US?

Yes. Your $8 USD monthly membership includes worldwide postage. Available destinations and delivery estimates will be confirmed before subscriptions open.

When will my first envelope ship?

The first mailing date and monthly signup cutoff will be announced before paid subscriptions open. International delivery times will vary.

How do I manage my membership?

Once subscriptions open, the member portal will let you manage billing and cancellation. Address-change instructions will be provided with your welcome information.

Is the launch list a subscription?

No. Joining the launch list is free and does not reserve a founders print or charge your card. We will send you the signup details when paid membership opens.

A note on your email.

The launch list collects your email address so Pulp can send you the mail club launch announcement. It does not collect card or shipping details. Launch emails will include a way to leave the list. For a removal request before launch, contact @pulp.sm on Instagram.
